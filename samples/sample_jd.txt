Senior Backend Engineer - Payments Platform

About the role
You will own core services in our payments platform: the ledger, the
settlement engine, and the reconciliation pipeline. This is a hands-on
senior IC role with significant design ownership.

Requirements
- 5+ years of backend engineering experience in production systems
- Strong Go or Python; you will work in both
- Deep experience with distributed systems: consistency models, idempotency,
  exactly-once semantics, failure recovery
- Production experience with event streaming (Kafka, Pulsar, or similar)
- Strong relational database skills including schema design, indexing, and
  query optimisation at scale
- Experience operating services in production: on-call, incident response,
  observability
- Track record of designing systems that others build on

Nice to have
- Payments, ledger, or fintech domain experience
- Kubernetes and infrastructure-as-code
- Experience mentoring engineers

What you will do
- Design and build the next generation of our ledger service
- Improve settlement latency and throughput
- Partner with compliance on audit and reconciliation requirements
- Mentor mid-level engineers on the team
